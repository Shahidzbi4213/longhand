# Welcome to longhand

Hi. This is longhand.

I made it because I was writing a book, and every Markdown app I tried got in the way. Too many panels. Too many buttons. Not enough page.

So longhand is just a page. Your words, a quiet bar at the bottom, and nothing else unless you ask for it.

This note takes 3 minutes to read. Promise.

## Three ways to see your writing

- **Write** (⌘1): the page itself. Markdown stays visible but faded, so you can see how things are formatted without the clutter.
- **Split** (⌘2): the source on the left, the finished page on the right. They scroll together.
- **Preview** (⌘3): only the finished page. Good for reading a chapter the way a reader will.

## Type / for anything

Start a line with `/` and a small menu appears: headings, lists, quotes, images, footnotes, tables, and Leanpub bits like warnings, tips and page breaks. Keep typing to filter, press Return to insert.

## The quiet bar

Look at the bottom of the window: text size, fonts, word count, theme. Click any word to change it. Click **Random** when you want a different feel.

The bar fades away as soon as you start typing and comes back when you move the mouse.

## Disappear completely

**Distraction-free** (⌘⇧D) hides everything (sidebar, toolbar, bar) and goes fullscreen. Just you and the page. Press ⌘⇧D again to come back.

Two more for deep work:

- **Focus mode** (⌘⇧F) dims every paragraph except the one you are in.
- **Typewriter** (⌘⇧T) keeps your current line in the middle of the screen.

## Pick your paper

Light, Paper, Sepia, Dark, or System to follow your Mac. Click the theme name in the bar to cycle through them, or press ⌥⌘1 to ⌥⌘4.

Fonts include iA Writer Quattro, Duo and Mono, Lato, New York, Charter and Georgia.

## Made for books

- Open a whole folder (⌘O) and every chapter shows up in the sidebar, images too.
- Images appear right on the page: `![caption](Images/cover.png)`.
- Leanpub / Markua works: `W>` warnings, `T>` tips, `{width: 60%}` above images, `{pagebreak}`, and footnotes[^1].
- Notes to yourself like [TODO: check this number] are highlighted, and the bar counts how many are left to fix.

That's it. Everything saves on its own.
